Agentic AI Systems

IBM SkillsBuild · Jino Jacob Sojan · Issued April 1, 2026

Certification Overview

From responses to workflows

Agentic AI covers systems that can plan, use tools, and perform connected steps toward a goal.

Agent Concepts

Planning, actions and observations

  • Break a task into steps
  • Select suitable actions or tools
  • Use results to determine the next step
Tool Use

Connecting agents to capabilities

  • Use external tools when appropriate
  • Coordinate multiple steps
  • Keep tool permissions and outputs in scope
Agent Orchestration

Managing complex workflows

  • Plan sequences of actions
  • Track progress and context
  • Handle failures and review outcomes
Safety & Evaluation

Reliability matters

  • Evaluate goal completion
  • Consider constraints and human oversight
  • Monitor accuracy, safety and performance
